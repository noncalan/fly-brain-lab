# Neuron sets

Every stimulus and readout in Fly Brain Lab is a named set of FlyWire v783 root IDs in `data/neuron_sets.json`,
generated by `tools/neuron-sets/build_sets.py`. No set may be added without a source in this table.

Sources:

* **[A]** FlyWire annotations, `flyconnectome/flywire_annotations@a83b2776`, release v3.2.0,
  `Supplemental_file1_neuron_annotations.tsv` (Schlegel et al., Nature 2024, 10.1038/s41586-024-07686-5, and later
  releases; see that repo's citation section).
* **[S]** Shiu et al. code, `philshiu/Drosophila_brain_model@91bdd1e7`, `figures.ipynb` / `example.ipynb` (MIT).
  IDs there are FlyWire v630; only IDs that still exist in v783 are kept.
* **[F]** The flybrain fork (`data/neurons.csv.gz` defines the v783 neuron list).

"Probe" = mean rate of the readout in the Shiu-parameter port, 150 Hz input (`docs/MODEL_NOTES.md` §3,
`tools/probe/results/`). It is evidence that the model responds, not yet a validated number.

| Set id | Behaviour | Rule | n | Source |

| `mdn` | Backward walking (candidate) | `cell_type = MDN` | 4 | A — role from literature, **not yet allowed**, see below |
| `dnp09` | Forward walking (candidate) | `cell_type = DNp09` | 2 | A — role from literature, **not yet allowed** |
| `dna02` | Turning (candidate) | `cell_type = DNa02` | 2 | A — role from literature, **not yet allowed** |

The FlyWire annotation gives identity (cell type, class, side), not behaviour. The behavioural role of MN9, aDN1 and
aBN1 is stated in Shiu et al.; Giant Fiber's role is in its annotated name. For MDN, DNp09, DNa02 the role comes
from other papers (not one of the three allowed sources), so they are listed but not wired to a behaviour until Nurik
approves the extra source.

## Not available

| Requested | Why |
|---|---|
| Nociception (true nociceptors) | No nociceptor class in the FAFB brain annotations (`cell_class` search for "noci": 0). Fork's `NOCI` group has 0 neurons. |
| Mechanical touch on body / legs | Body and leg mechanosensors project to the VNC, which FAFB does not contain. Only head/antennal bristles and JO exist. |
| Withdrawal (отдёргивание) | Leg/proboscis retraction motor neurons are in the VNC or not annotated with that role. |
| Freezing | No annotated brain neuron with that role in the allowed sources. |
| Walking | Leg motor neurons are in the VNC; candidate DNs above need an extra literature source. |
| Leg grooming | VNC-dependent; only antennal grooming (aDN1/aBN1) is supported. |
